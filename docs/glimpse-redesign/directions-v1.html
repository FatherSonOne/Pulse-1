Pulse · Glimpse Section · Direction Exploration v1

Three differentiated ways the Glimpse inbox could earn the depth of what's behind it.

The Glimpse chat view already reads like a decision document — AI summaries, action items, topic chips, transcript-on-demand. The conversations list it lives behind still reads like 2018 SMS. These three directions explore what closing that asymmetry could look like, each from a different design tradition. Same sample data, same coral-as-signal contract, same 11px-mono system signature. Picking one is the next step.

Direction 01

Triage Cockpit

Pentagram info-architecture × Tufte data density

The conversations list becomes a triage table. Every row surfaces what the AI already knows: a two-line summary peek, action-item count, duration, and a relative timestamp — all aligned to a column-header strip in the system's 11px mono. You decide what to watch by reading, not by guessing.

The header collapses to title plus a single primary CTA; everything secondary moves into an overflow. Coral stays disciplined: unread dot, action-item pill, the AI provenance chip, and the Record CTA. Empty state is a quiet enterprise-style stamp, not a marketing card.

  • Risk: reads "enterprise SaaS" — could feel cold to a user expecting a friendly inbox.
  • Risk: 5-column grid demands a careful mobile collapse — likely 2 rows per item under 480px.
Pick this if Pulse leans into "decision-document" as its identity — and the inbox should feel like a triage console, not a chat list.

Direction 02

Decision Digest

Kenya Hara emptiness × Anthropic editorial restraint

The conversations list reads as a daily reading digest. Each item gets room to breathe — the sender becomes a typographic byline, the AI summary becomes the headline, and the existing chrome (action counts, kind chips, timestamps) demotes to quiet metadata that sits below the summary.

Big white space between rows. Header is just a title and a hairline link; no icon-only buttons fighting for attention. The empty state is one editorial sentence with a tiny coral text link — the opposite of a marketing card. Coral stays disciplined: unread dot, AI provenance, the single Record link, and the action-count number.

  • Risk: low density — when the inbox is busy, you scroll. May not work for power users.
  • Risk: action items risk getting buried below the summary copy; needs A/B against a higher-density variant.
  • Risk: "editorial digest" energy conflicts with the cockpit-state vocabulary the rest of Pulse leans into.
Pick this if Pulse wants Glimpse to feel like a daily briefing — something you read, not something you triage.

Direction 03

Transmission Feed

Field.io motion poetics × cockpit/terminal industrial

The list becomes a feed of transmissions. Each row is split into three columns: a 4px state stripe (coral when unread), a 64px stamp rail showing the sender + AI + viewed glyphs in vertical stack, and a transmission readout with summary + topic tags. A right-edge meter shows duration and arrival time in tabular mono.

The header is a monospace coordinates row — "Inbox · 14 signals · 6 unread" — that acknowledges the section's positioning as Pulse's signal layer. Empty state is a quiet terminal stamp ("NO INCOMING TRANSMISSIONS") with a coral BROADCAST CTA. Coral discipline: state stripe, AI glyph, broadcast CTA, the existing AI provenance chip.

  • Risk: niche aesthetic — pulls Pulse toward cockpit/spy-thriller energy, which not every user will read as "professional".
  • Risk: stamp rail competes for attention with the readout; needs careful icon weights.
  • Risk: "BROADCAST" language conflicts with the chat-app metaphor casual users carry in.
Pick this if Pulse wants to push its cockpit identity further — and Glimpse should feel like the section where the signal traffic actually shows up.
