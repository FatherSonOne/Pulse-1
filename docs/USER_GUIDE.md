## 1. Introduction

### What is Pulse?

Pulse is your all-in-one communication and productivity hub. Instead of juggling separate apps for email, text, voice messages, team chat, meetings, calendars, and CRM data, Pulse brings everything into one place — with smart AI tools built in to help you stay organized, respond faster, and make better decisions.

Think of Pulse as your unified communication command center: every message, every meeting, every task — one app, one inbox, one view.

### Who is Pulse for?

Pulse is built for:

- **Professionals** who manage large volumes of email, messages, and meetings
- **Teams** that need to coordinate, vote on decisions, and track tasks together
- **Customer-facing roles** who rely on CRM tools like HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive
- **Remote workers** who need seamless voice, video, and chat without switching apps
- **Managers** who need visibility into team workload, decisions, and communication health
- **Anyone** who wants to communicate smarter without switching between a dozen apps

### Key Concepts

| Term | What it means |
| ------ | --------------- |
| **Workspace** | Your personal or team environment within Pulse — your isolated space for all data and settings |
| **Unified Inbox** | A single inbox showing messages from all your connected channels in one view |
| **Channel** | A source of messages: Email, SMS, Slack, or internal Pulse messages |
| **Contact** | Any person you communicate with, pulled from all your connected accounts |
| **Voxer** | Pulse's voice messaging system — async voice with AI transcription and summaries |
| **Pulse AI** | The context-aware AI assistant available throughout the entire app |

## 2. Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Open Pulse in your browser or install the mobile app.
2. Click **Sign Up** on the welcome screen.
3. Choose how you'd like to create your account:
   - **Sign in with Google** — recommended; auto-imports contacts and calendar
   - **Sign in with Microsoft** — for Microsoft 365 users; imports Outlook and Teams contacts
   - **Email & Password** — manual account creation, requires email verification
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to verify your email if signing up manually.
5. You'll land on your Dashboard once your account is ready.

**Tip:** Using **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft** automatically connects your contacts and calendar during setup — saving significant setup time.

### Setting Up Your Profile

1. Click your avatar or name in the bottom-left of the sidebar.
2. Select **Settings → Profile**.
3. Add your name, profile photo, job title, and time zone.
4. Optionally add your phone number for SMS notifications.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

**Tip:** A complete profile with a photo makes it easier for teammates to recognize you in decisions, tasks, and meeting invites.

### Connecting Your Accounts

After creating your account, connect your existing tools to unlock the full power of Pulse:

1. Go to **Settings → Connected Accounts**.
2. Connect any combination of:
   - **Google** — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts
   - **Microsoft** — Outlook email, Microsoft Calendar, Microsoft contacts
   - **Slack** — Slack messages appear in your Unified Inbox
   - **CRM** — HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho
   - **SMS** — Your phone number for text messaging
3. Each connection shows a green "Connected" badge when active.

### Navigating Pulse — The Sidebar

The left sidebar is your main navigation. It is organized into sections:

**Primary Section** (top):

- **Dashboard** — Your home view with summaries and quick actions
- **Messages** — Your unified inbox across all channels
- **Email** — Full email client
- **SMS** — Text messaging
- **Voxer** — Voice messaging hub
- **Calendar** — Your schedule
- **Meetings** — Video meetings, templates, and meeting history

**Secondary Section** (middle):

- **Contacts** — Your contact directory with relationship insights
- **Decisions & Tasks** — Collaborative decision-making and task tracking
- **Studio** — AI research, analysis, and content workspace
- **Tools** — Automation, AI Lab, and power tools
- **Analytics** — Reports, predictions, and communication metrics
- **Archives** — Stored sessions, transcripts, decisions, and documents

**Bottom**:

- **AI Assistant** — The live ECG waveform icon — click it or press `Ctrl+/`
- **Settings** — Preferences and connected accounts
- **Your profile** — Quick access to account options

**Tip:** Click the arrow button at the top of the sidebar to collapse it and give yourself more screen space. Press `G` then a letter to jump directly to any section.

**Tip:** Click the **?** button in the sidebar header (or on mobile, between the notifications bell and the menu icon) to open the User's Guide at any time without leaving your current view.

## 3. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see when you open Pulse. It gives you a real-time summary of everything important at a glance, so you can start your day informed without digging through multiple apps.

### What You See

The Dashboard is organized into several widgets, each focused on a different slice of your day:

- **Daily Overview** — A full-width AI-generated briefing at the top of the Dashboard. Pulse reads your calendar, tasks, messages, email, and voice messages and produces a personalized summary every morning (and refreshes every 5 minutes). Includes a greeting, a written summary, Key Highlights, Action Items, and a Focus Recommendation.
- **AI Web Search Bar** — A full-width search bar below the Daily Overview. Type any question and Pulse searches the web using AI, returning a summary with source links.
- **Quick Stats Cards** — Unread messages, pending tasks, today's meetings, and recent activity counts. Click any card to jump directly to that section.
- **Mini Pulse AI** — An always-visible AI assistant widget on your Dashboard. Use quick-tap chips ("Summarize my day", "What's urgent?", "Draft a reply") or type any question. Responses appear inline — click **Open Pulse AI** to expand into the full AI assistant.
- **Upcoming Events** — Your next 3 calendar events with a live countdown badge showing how far away each one is (e.g., *in 2h*, *in 15m*, *now*). Click **View all** to open your full calendar.
- **Unread Pulse** — A quick-glance widget showing your unread count for Messages, Email, and Vox. Click any row to jump directly to that section and see what's waiting.
- **Quick Scheduler** — Create a meeting or appointment without leaving the Dashboard.
- **AI Nudges** — Banners for overdue tasks, stalled decisions, follow-up reminders, and meeting prep alerts.

### Daily Overview — Your AI Morning Briefing

The **Daily Overview** is the first thing you see on your Dashboard. Every time you open Pulse (and every 5 minutes while it's open), the AI reads across all your connected channels — calendar, tasks, messages, email, and voice messages — and writes you a personalized briefing.

**What's in the Daily Overview:**

- **Greeting** — A time-aware greeting (Good morning, Good afternoon, etc.) with the current context.
- **Summary** — 1–2 sentences summing up your day: what's on, what's urgent, what needs attention.
- **Focus Recommendation** — A highlighted suggestion for the single most important thing to tackle first. Click it to jump directly to that item.
- **Quick Stats** — Four at-a-glance numbers: Unread messages, Pending tasks, Today's meetings, Unplayed Voxes.
- **Key Highlights** — Up to 4 cards pulling the most important items from your calendar, tasks, messages, and email. Click any card to go straight to that item.
- **Action Items** — Up to 4 smart suggestions for things you should do today. Each has an action button (reply, calendar, task, message, microphone, etc.) — click it to act immediately.

**How to use it:**

1. Open Pulse — the Daily Overview loads automatically at the top of your Dashboard.
2. Read the greeting and summary for an instant picture of your day.
3. Click the **Focus Recommendation** to jump to your most important item right away.
4. Scan the **Key Highlights** cards — click any to go directly to that item.
5. Work through the **Action Items** — click the button on the right of each card to take action.
6. Click the **refresh icon** (top right of the briefing) to regenerate the briefing at any time.

**Tip:** The briefing refreshes automatically every 5 minutes. If you just got a new email or completed a task, click the refresh icon to get an updated view immediately.

**Tip:** If you haven't connected your calendar or email yet, the briefing will be minimal. The more accounts you connect in Settings → Connected Accounts, the richer and more useful your briefing becomes.

### Using the AI Web Search Bar

1. Click the search bar at the top of the Dashboard.
2. Type any question — e.g., "What is the current EUR/USD rate?" or "Best practices for onboarding remote employees."
3. Press **Enter** or click **Search**.
4. Pulse returns an AI-written summary with up to 3 source links you can open.
5. Click **Dismiss** to clear the result and return to the normal Dashboard view.

**Tip:** Use the web search bar for quick market research, competitive intel, or any question you'd normally open a new browser tab for.

### Using Mini Pulse AI

1. Scroll to the **Ask Pulse AI** widget on your Dashboard.
2. Tap one of the quick chips to ask a common question instantly, or type your own question in the input box.
3. Press **Enter** — a response appears inline below the input.
4. For a longer conversation or more detailed answers, click **Open Pulse AI →** to open the full AI assistant.
5. Click **Clear** to dismiss the response.

### Using the Quick Scheduler

1. Click **+ Schedule** in the Quick Scheduler widget.
2. Enter a title, date, time, and invite guests by name or email.
3. Click **Create** — the event is added to your calendar instantly.
4. Invited guests receive a calendar invite by email.

### Dashboard AI Nudges

The Pulse AI monitors your workspace and surfaces important items on the Dashboard:

- **Overdue tasks** — Highlighted with a red badge
- **Stalled decisions** — Decisions awaiting your vote
- **Follow-up reminders** — Contacts you haven't reached out to recently
- **Meeting prep alerts** — Prep cards for meetings starting within the next hour

Click any nudge to jump directly to the relevant item. Click **Dismiss** to clear it.

**Tip:** The Dashboard refreshes automatically. You don't need to reload the page to see new messages or events.

- **Upcoming Events** pulls from your connected calendars — connect your calendar in Settings → Connected Accounts to populate this.

## 4. Unified Messaging

The **Messages** section is your central inbox for all conversations — internal team messages, direct messages, and external communications from all your connected channels.

### Reading Messages

1. Click **Messages** in the sidebar.
2. Your inbox shows all messages across all channels, sorted by most recent.
3. Click any conversation to open it in the reading panel on the right.
4. Unread messages are marked with a blue dot on the left.

### Replying to a Message

1. Open a conversation.
2. Click the text box at the bottom of the conversation.
3. Type your reply.
4. Press **Enter** to send, or **Shift+Enter** for a new line without sending.

### Composing a New Message

1. Click the **Compose** button (pencil icon) at the top of the Messages panel.
2. Type a recipient name or email in the **To** field — Pulse auto-suggests from your contacts.
3. Write your message using the rich text toolbar (bold, italic, lists, links).
4. Click **Send**.

### Inline Tools — The `/` Command Menu

While typing in any message box, press `/` to open the Inline Tools menu for quick access to:

- **/gif** — Insert a GIF from the built-in library
- **/file** — Attach a file from your device
- **/task** — Create a task directly from the message
- **/poll** — Insert a quick poll for your team to respond to
- **/reminder** — Set a reminder for yourself about this message

### Message Reactions

- Hover over any message and click the smiley face icon.
- Pick an emoji from the picker.
- The reaction appears below the message and others can add their reactions too.
- Hover over a reaction to see who reacted.

### Pinning Messages

- Hover over a message and click the **pin icon**.
- Pinned messages appear in a special pinned section at the top of the conversation.
- Click **View Pinned** to see all pinned items at once.
- Useful for keeping track of decisions, links, and key information in busy threads.

### Thread Conversations

- Click **Reply in Thread** on any message to start a side conversation.
- Threads keep the main channel clean — replies stay organized in their own space.
- Thread replies appear in the sidebar and in the **Threads** pane.
- You receive a notification when someone replies to a thread you're following.

### Scheduling a Message

1. Click the **clock icon** next to the send button.
2. Pick a date and time for delivery.
3. Click **Schedule Send** — your message will be sent automatically at that time.
4. Scheduled messages appear in a **Scheduled** folder — you can edit or cancel them before they send.

### Smart Folders & Labels

Pulse automatically organizes your messages into smart folders:

- **Priority** — Messages flagged as urgent by AI
- **Team** — Messages from your workspace team members
- **Follow-ups** — Messages that need a response from you
- **Archived** — Conversations you've filed away

You can also create custom labels: right-click any conversation and select **Add Label**. Labels can be colored for quick visual scanning.

### Searching Messages

1. Click the **search bar** at the top of the Messages panel.
2. Type any keyword, person name, or phrase.
3. Results appear instantly across all your connected channels.
4. Press `Enter` to load full results in the Search panel with filtering options.

**Tip:** Use quotes for exact phrases: `"quarterly review"` finds only that exact text.

### Bulk Actions

- Check the box next to multiple conversations (or press `Ctrl+A` to select all visible).
- Choose from: **Archive**, **Mark as Read**, **Delete**, or **Add Label**.
- Great for cleaning up your inbox quickly after a busy period.

### Sentiment Timeline

For any long conversation, click **Insights → Sentiment Timeline** to see a visual graph of how the tone of the conversation has changed over time — useful for detecting friction or spotting when things got positive.

### Conversation Highlights

Click **Highlights** on any conversation to see a quick summary of:
- Key decisions made in the thread
- Action items mentioned
- Important dates referenced
- Commitments made by each person

### Ecosystem Bot Messages

When you have connected apps via the **Ecosystem Bridge** (see Section 17), those apps can post bot messages directly into your channels. Bot messages look different from regular messages — they have a colored bot avatar and a distinct card layout depending on the message type:

- **Task Assigned cards** (from Entomate) — show a task title, priority level, assignee, and due date. Click **View Task** to open the task in Entomate directly.
- **Meeting Recap cards** (from Entomate or Logos Vision) — show a meeting summary with sentiment analysis, key decisions, quality score, and extracted action items. Click any action item to open it.
- **Meeting Briefing cards** — show pre-meeting context: participant history, open action items, and context highlights. These appear before upcoming meetings so you walk in prepared.

Bot messages appear in the channel alongside regular messages but are visually distinct. They cannot be replied to directly — use the action buttons on the card to respond.

**Tip:** Bot messages flow into dedicated bot channels (like #entomate-meetings) if you've set them up in the Ecosystem Bridge settings. Otherwise, they appear in your general message feed.

**Tip:** Bot messages are sent by the ecosystem app, not by a person — you will see a bot name ("Entomate" or "Logos Vision") and a robot icon instead of a user avatar.

## 5. Email

Pulse includes a full-featured email client, so you never need to switch to a separate email app. Your Gmail or Outlook inbox is fully accessible inside Pulse alongside all your other channels.

### Connecting Your Email Account

1. Go to **Settings → Connected Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect Email**.
3. Choose **Google (Gmail)** or **Microsoft (Outlook)**.
4. Sign in and grant the required permissions.
5. Your emails will start syncing within a few minutes — the process runs in the background.

**Tip:** You can connect multiple email accounts (e.g., work Gmail and a personal Outlook) and switch between them within the Email view.

### Reading Email

1. Click **Email** in the sidebar.
2. Your inbox is on the left — click any email to read it on the right.
3. Emails are organized by folder: Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Spam, Trash.
4. AI automatically identifies emails that need action and surfaces them at the top.

### Composing Email

1. Click **Compose** (the pencil icon at the top).
2. Fill in **To**, **CC/BCC**, and **Subject**.
3. Write your message using the rich text toolbar: bold, italic, links, lists, images, and file attachments.
4. Click **Send** — or schedule it (see below).

### Daily Briefing

Pulse generates an **AI Daily Briefing** email summary that appears at the top of your inbox each morning. It includes:

- Your top 5 priority emails needing a response
- Action items extracted from recent emails
- Important senders who haven't heard back from you
- Meetings referenced in email threads

Click any item in the briefing to jump directly to that email.

### Email Templates

Save time by using pre-written email templates:

1. While composing, click the **Templates** icon in the toolbar.
2. Choose a template from your saved list.
3. The template text is inserted — edit as needed before sending.

To create a new template:
1. Go to **Settings → Email Templates**.
2. Click **New Template**, write it, and save.
3. Use variables like `{{first_name}}` and `{{company}}` to auto-personalize.

### Scheduling an Email

1. While composing, click the **clock icon** next to Send.
2. Pick a date and time, or choose a smart option: **Tomorrow morning**, **Monday 9 AM**, **Next week**.
3. Click **Schedule** — the email queues in your **Scheduled** folder.
4. Edit or cancel it at any time before it sends.

### Email Filters & Folders

Auto-organize your inbox with custom rules:

1. Go to **Settings → Email Filters**.
2. Click **New Filter**.
3. Set **conditions** — e.g., From: `boss@company.com` or Subject contains `[Invoice]`.
4. Set **actions** — e.g., Move to "Finance", Mark as Important, Apply label "Client".
5. Click **Save** — the filter runs on all new matching emails.

**Tip:** Use filters to create a "VIP" folder for your most important senders so their emails are never buried.

### Follow-up Reminders

Never let an important email go unanswered:

1. When reading an email, click **Set Follow-up Reminder**.
2. Pick a time: In 1 hour, Tomorrow, In 2 days, or a custom date.
3. If the thread is still quiet when the reminder fires, Pulse notifies you to reply.
4. Dismiss the reminder if you've already handled it.

### Confidential Emails

For sensitive communications:

1. While composing, click the **lock icon** in the toolbar.
2. Mark as **Confidential**.
3. Recipients see a notice that the email is confidential and it is not forwarded automatically.

### AI Action Items from Email

When reading an email, click **Extract Action Items**:

1. Pulse reads the entire email thread.
2. It identifies tasks, deadlines, commitments, and requests.
3. A list appears — review and click **Add to Tasks** to save selected items.
4. Each extracted task includes the email source as context.

### Email Campaigns

Email Campaigns let you send a single professionally-written email to an audience segment — all your contacts, your VIP contacts, recently active contacts, or a custom group you define.

#### The Campaigns Dashboard

1. Click **Email** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Campaigns** in the Email navigation bar.
3. The dashboard shows all your draft, scheduled, and sent campaigns with status, recipient count, and send date.
4. Use the **search bar** to find campaigns by name or subject.
5. Click the **⋮ menu** on any campaign to **Duplicate** or **Delete** it.

#### Creating a Campaign

1. Click **New Campaign** from the Campaigns dashboard.
2. The Campaign Builder opens as a 3-step wizard.

**Step 1 — Setup:**

- Enter a campaign name (internal only, recipients do not see this) and your From Name.
- Choose an **Audience Segment** from your saved segments — Pulse automatically loads the matching recipients.
- Or paste email addresses manually in the Recipients field.

**Step 2 — Compose:**

- Write your **Subject Line** and optional preview text.
- Write the body using the rich text editor.
- Click **AI Draft** — describe what you want and choose a tone (Professional, Friendly, Persuasive, or Concise). Pulse writes a full draft instantly.
- Click **Templates** to insert a saved email template and edit it.
- Your campaign **auto-saves as a draft** every few seconds — you will never lose your work.

**Step 3 — Review & Send:**

- Preview the final email and verify the recipient list.
- Click **Send Now** to send immediately, or click **Schedule** to pick a future date and time.

#### Audience Segments

Audience Segments are saved groups of contacts that you can reuse across any campaign.

### Advanced Search Tips

| Query | What it finds |
| ------- | --------------- |
| `"exact phrase"` | Only results with that exact phrase |
| `from:John` | Messages or emails sent by John |
| `to:sarah` | Messages sent to Sarah |
| `after:2026-01-01` | Content created after that date |
| `before:2026-03-01` | Content created before that date |
| `type:email` | Email results only |
| `type:task` | Tasks only |
| `#tag` | Content with that label or tag |
| `has:attachment` | Emails or messages with attachments |

### Geo Modifiers — Search Near a Place

Append a geographic constraint to any search to filter results by physical location. The parser runs on every keystroke, so you see results narrow as you type.

**Recognized forms:**

| Query example | What it finds |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| `coffee meeting near me` | Results within 25 miles of your current location (default radius) |
| `coffee meeting near me within 5 mi` | Results within 5 miles of you |
| `event near Austin` | Results near Austin (default 25 mi) |
| `client near San Francisco within 10 mi` | Results within 10 mi of San Francisco |
| `vendor within 50 km of Berlin` | Results within 50 km of Berlin (km auto-converted to miles) |
| `dinner within 15 mi` | Results within 15 mi of you (bare radius implies "near me") |

When a geo modifier is active, an **active filter chip** appears above the results showing the radius and place — e.g., **Near Austin · 25 mi**. Click the chip to remove the filter.

**How it works:**

- Pulse geocodes the place using Google Places.
- Any result that has a place attached (contact location, event venue, task location, decision venue) is checked against the radius.
- Results without a place are still shown unless you explicitly filter by location-having content.

### Map View — See Search Results on a Map

Switch the search results layout to **Map View** to plot location-bearing results geographically.

1. Run any search — with or without a geo modifier.
2. Click the **Map** button in the view-mode toggle (alongside List, Grid, and Timeline).
3. Each result with a place appears as a pin; click any pin to open the result inline.
4. Combine with a geo modifier (e.g., `client meetings near me within 25 mi`) to see exactly what's nearby.

**Tip:** Map view pairs with the Coral Cockpit Contact Map — both share Pulse's universal Place schema, so a place attached to one entity appears everywhere.

### Search Alerts

Set up alerts for specific search queries:

1. Run a search you want to monitor.
2. Click **Set Alert** in the results bar.
3. Choose alert frequency: Real-time, Daily digest, or Weekly digest.
4. Pulse notifies you when new results match your saved search.

**Example:** Set an alert for "from:bigclient.com" — get notified instantly whenever your key client emails you.

## 22. Troubleshooting & FAQ

### Messages aren't loading

1. Check your internet connection — try opening another website.
2. Refresh the page (`F5` or `Ctrl+R`).
3. Log out and log back in.
4. Clear your browser cache and reload.
5. Try a different browser — Chrome is recommended.
6. If the problem continues, check the Pulse status page.

### Email isn't syncing

1. Go to **Settings → Connected Accounts → Email**.
2. Check the sync status — it should say "Connected."
3. If it says "Error" or "Disconnected," click **Reconnect**.
4. Re-authenticate with your Google or Microsoft account.
5. Make sure you granted Pulse all required permissions during reconnection — don't skip any permission dialog.

**Tip:** If reconnecting doesn't work, disconnect the account fully, wait 30 seconds, then connect it again from scratch.

### Not receiving notifications

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications** — verify notifications are enabled for the relevant channel.
2. Check that your **browser allows notifications** for Pulse (browser address bar → Site settings → Notifications → Allow).
3. Check that **Quiet Hours** aren't active during the time you expect notifications.
4. On mobile: go to your phone's System Settings → Apps → Pulse → Notifications → ensure they're enabled.

### Can't hear Voxer messages

1. Check that your device volume is not muted.
2. Ensure your browser has **microphone and audio permissions** for Pulse (address bar → Site settings).
3. Try a different browser — Chrome is recommended.
4. On mobile: go to System Settings → Apps → Pulse → Permissions → Microphone → Allow.

### Voxer recording won't start

1. Make sure no other app is using your microphone (close other browser tabs with audio).
2. Click the lock icon in your browser address bar → Site settings → Microphone → Allow.
3. Try refreshing the page and granting microphone access again when prompted.
4. On Windows: check Privacy settings → Microphone access → ensure browser is allowed.

### A contact appears twice

1. Go to **Contacts → Tools → Find Duplicates**.
2. Locate the duplicate pair.
3. Click **Merge** to combine them — data from both records is preserved.
4. If Pulse doesn't flag them automatically, search for the contact and manually select "Merge with…" from the contact's options menu.

### Calendar events not showing

1. Go to **Settings → Connected Accounts → Calendar** and confirm your calendar is connected.
2. Click **Sync Now** to force an immediate sync.
3. Check that you're viewing the correct calendar — click the calendar filter to confirm the right calendar is shown.
4. Make sure you haven't hidden any calendars in the view filter.

### CRM data not appearing on contacts

1. Go to **Settings → Connected Accounts → CRM** and confirm the connection is active.
2. Check the last sync time — if it's more than an hour ago, click **Sync Now**.
3. Open a contact and look for the **CRM panel** on their profile — it only appears for contacts that exist in your CRM.
4. If the contact was recently added to the CRM, wait 15 minutes for the next sync.

### How do I delete my account?

1. Go to **Settings → Data Management**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **Delete Account**.
3. Follow the confirmation steps — you'll be asked to type "DELETE" to confirm.
4. Your account and all data are permanently deleted within 30 days.

> **Warning:** Deleting your account is permanent. Export your data first if you may need it.

### How do I change my workspace name?

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace → General**.
2. Update the workspace name and/or logo.
3. Click **Save** — the change applies immediately for all workspace members.

### How do I invite someone to my workspace?

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace → Members**.
2. Click **Invite Member**.
3. Enter their email address and select their role (Member, Admin, Guest).
4. Click **Send Invite** — they receive an email with a join link.

### How do I get help?

- Click the **?** icon in the sidebar for in-app contextual help
- Email support from **Settings → Help & Support → Contact Support**
- Browse the knowledge base from the help panel for step-by-step articles

### Reporting a Bug

1. Click **Settings → Help & Support → Report a Bug**.
2. Describe what happened, what you expected to happen, and the steps to reproduce.
3. Include a screenshot or screen recording if possible — this significantly speeds up resolution.
4. Click **Submit** — our team reviews and follows up within 1 business day.

## 23. Summit — Live Voice (Experimental)

Summit is a real-time voice agent you can speak to naturally. It listens, transcribes, summarizes, and pulls out decisions and tasks while you talk — solo, with a teammate, or while thinking out loud.

You'll find it in the sidebar under the **Experimental** section. It's a new way to use Pulse — instead of typing, you just talk.

### Starting a Session

1. Open **Summit** from the sidebar (or press `Ctrl+/` and search "Summit").
2. On first visit, read the explainer modal — it tells you which mode you're in (BYO key, plan minutes, or trial) and your monthly minute budget.
3. Click the microphone button to start.
4. The header meter (e.g., "12 / 60 MIN") tracks usage in real time and color-shifts at 80% (amber) and 100% (red).
5. Speak naturally. Summit transcribes both sides and shows a live transcript rail.
6. When you mention a decision, action item, or follow-up, Summit captures it into the **Artifacts panel** on the right.
7. Pause or mute with the header controls — the meter pauses with you.
8. End the session with the **X** button. The End Session sheet lets you push transcripts, decisions, tasks, or summaries to Messages, Decisions, Tasks, War Room, or Email.

### Two Modes — BYO Key vs Plan Minutes

Summit runs in one of two modes.

- **BYO Key.** Paste your own OpenAI API key in **Settings → AI Providers**. Summit uses it directly, you pay OpenAI, and there's no Pulse minute cap.
- **Plan minutes.** Your Pulse plan grants a monthly minute pool plus a per-session length cap.

Switch modes anytime by adding or removing your key. Pulse picks BYO automatically when a valid key is on file.

> **Note:** You don't need a key for normal Pulse AI features (summaries, smart replies, transcription). The BYO key is *only* for Summit live voice.

### Plan Limits

| Plan | Monthly Minutes | Per-Session Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Trial | 15 min | 5 min |
| Pulse Team | 60 min | 15 min |
| Pulse Growth | 240 min | 30 min |
| BYO OpenAI Key | Unlimited | Unlimited |

When you reach the per-session cap, Summit warns at 60 seconds remaining and hard-disconnects at zero — your transcript is preserved. When you exhaust the monthly minutes, the meter turns red and **Start** is disabled until next billing cycle (or until you add a BYO key).

### Artifacts — What Summit Captures

Summit watches the transcript and surfaces actionable moments in the right-hand Artifacts panel:

- **Decision** — when you say "let's go with X" or "we decided Y".
- **Task** — when you say "I need to email Sam by Friday" (assignee, deadline, and source quote captured).
- **Question** — when something needs follow-up.
- **Summary** — click **Summarize so far** at any time for a structured recap.

Each artifact has Edit and Delete controls — review before sending anywhere.

### Ending and Exporting

1. Click **X** or press `Esc` to open the End Session sheet.
2. Pick destinations: **Messages**, **Decisions**, **Tasks**, **War Room**, or **Email**.
3. Each destination shows a status indicator (queued, sent, failed).
4. You can also **Copy** the markdown transcript or **Download** it.
5. Sessions auto-save to **Archives → Voice Sessions** and to the Sessions panel inside Summit.

### Auto-Disconnect Safety

Summit protects you from runaway sessions:

- **60-second warning** before your session cap is reached.
- **Hard disconnect** at the cap — transcript is saved.
- **Tab-hidden pause** after 2 minutes; full disconnect after 10.
- **Inactivity disconnect** after 5 minutes with no detected speech.

Plan-minute mode never bills paused or background time — the meter only counts active spoken audio.

### Pro Tips

- **Tip:** Use Summit as a thinking partner — talk through a hard decision and let the artifact panel capture conclusions.
- **Tip:** For long research or brainstorming, add a BYO OpenAI key — it removes both the monthly cap and the per-session length cap.
- **Tip:** Pin the Summit tab while you work — tab-hidden pause keeps it from burning minutes when you switch away.
- **Tip:** Review the Artifacts panel before clicking End — edit out anything you don't want pushed.

### BYO Key Management

- Rotate your key anytime in **Settings → AI Providers** — Summit uses the new key on the next session.
- Remove the key to fall back to plan-included minutes — your minute usage that month is preserved.
- Set a monthly budget alarm in your OpenAI dashboard to be warned before unexpected charges.
- Keys are encrypted in Supabase Vault with strict access gating — only your own account can decrypt them server-side.

### Common Errors

| Error | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `NO_SUBSCRIPTION` | Your workspace is on Free | Upgrade to Team or Growth, or add a BYO key |
| `WRONG_TIER` | Your plan doesn't include Summit | Switch plan or use BYO key |
| `TRIAL_CAP` / `OVER_CAP` | All this month's minutes used | Wait for renewal or add a BYO key |
| `NOT_MEMBER` | You're not a workspace member | Ask your admin to re-invite you |
